# Zaroda Build: user guide

Zaroda Build runs a construction company: compliance, tenders, projects, site work, materials, money and wages.
Open it at **build.zarodasolutions.app** and sign in with your email and password.
Your **job** decides what you see. If something you need is missing, ask your Director to give you the role.
If a screen says **"Upgrade to use this"**, your company's plan does not include that feature. The Director or Accounts can change the plan under **Plan**.

On a phone, the **Site** menu has the screens built for site work: muster, diary, photos, safety and store.

---

## Director / Owner
You can do everything.
- **Home** shows every open project at once: contract value, certified, cost, margin, money owed by clients, retention held, who is on site today, and anything **behind programme** (in red).
- **Waiting for approval** lists LPOs, requisitions, valuations, variations and pay runs. Open the project to approve.
- **Staff & roles**: add people by their job, and set approval limits (Settings).
- You approve pay runs. You cannot approve one you prepared yourself.
- **Reports**: all eight reports. Download any of them as Excel, or print or save them as PDF.

## Contracts Manager
- Same overview on **Home** as the Director.
- Approve requisitions, LPOs (up to your limit), variations and valuations (then send them to the client's QS).
- Invite the client and consultants to the **Portal** (project → Portal tab).
- Reports: all eight, for every project.

## Project Manager
- Your projects only. Each project opens on its **Dashboard**: programme against time used, who is on site today, the day's diary, what waits for you, safety and money.
- Approve the site diary, requisitions and LPOs up to your limit. Record site instructions and DOSHS reports.
- Invite the client and consultants to the portal for your project.
- Reports cover your projects only.

## Quantity Surveyor (QS)
- BQ: import from Excel or type it in.
- Valuations: prepare each one from the BQ. The Contracts Manager approves it and sends it to the client's QS. Once certified, it is locked; a mistake is corrected with a reversing certificate.
- Variations, retention and subcontract accounts.
- Reports: cost against budget, cash flow, IPC & retention, materials, NCA.

## Accountant
- Invoice certified IPCs through eTIMS, and record payments and withholding certificates.
- **Payroll**: work out pay from the muster for a period. Someone else approves it. Then pay by M-Pesa, record cash, or record an M-Pesa payment made by hand. Workers get a payslip by SMS.
- Set the company's M-Pesa payout account under **Settings → Wages by M-Pesa**.
- Pay for the Zaroda plan (Plan).
- Tax rates (VAT, withholding, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, PAYE): add a new rate with the date it starts; old rates stay for past work.

## Procurement Officer
- Raise LPOs from approved requisitions. Register plant, and record hire and fuel.
- Reports: materials, compliance.

## Compliance Officer
- **Compliance** vault: NCA registration, licences, tax compliance certificate, insurance and permits, each with its expiry date. Alerts come 60, 30 and 7 days before anything expires.
- Staff NCA accreditation cards.
- Reports: compliance expiries, safety, NCA category use.

## Safety (SHE) Officer
- Safety registers on every project: toolbox talks, PPE, permits to work, incidents. A lost-time or fatal incident must be reported to DOSHS: the app reminds you before the deadline.
- Reports: safety statistics, compliance.

## Site Engineer and Site Supervisor (phone)
- **Site → Diary**: write the day's diary, add photos (the phone adds the location), then submit to the PM.
- **Site → Muster**: mark everyone present, half day or absent. It works with no signal and sends when the phone is back online.
- Raise requisitions for materials, and record safety talks, PPE and incidents.
- A Site Supervisor needs a valid NCA accreditation card.

## Storekeeper (phone)
- **Site → Store**: receive deliveries against LPOs (part deliveries are fine; record damaged items), issue materials to the works, and see stock on site.
- You see your project, but not the contract money or the client.

## Timekeeper (phone)
- **Site → Muster** and the worker register for your projects.

## Reports
Open **Reports** in the top bar. Pick a report, then a project (or all your projects) and dates if you want.
- **Excel** downloads a spreadsheet. **Print / PDF** opens a printable page; choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
- Mjengo and Fundi plans have the basic reports on screen. Cash flow, labour cost, NCA use and Excel/PDF exports need the Contractor plan or higher.
- If your plan has lapsed, the account is read-only, but you can still open and export every report.

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Project cost vs BQ budget | Materials delivered, labour, plant and subcontracts so far, against the budget and what is certified |
| Cash flow | Money in from IPCs and out on wages, subcontractors, materials and plant, month by month |
| IPC & retention | Certified, invoiced, received, withholding, outstanding and retention per project |
| Material reconciliation | BQ allowance, ordered, delivered, used, wasted, on site, and anything unaccounted for |
| Labour cost | Worker-days, labour cost, what is paid and still owed (no worker personal details) |
| Compliance expiries | Every document, licence, permit and card, soonest expiry first |
| Safety statistics | Incidents by type, DOSHS reports, toolbox talks, PPE, permits and the lost-time injury rate |
| NCA category use | Your NCA categories and caps against your biggest open contracts and tenders |

## Portal (clients, consultants, subcontractors)
People outside the company use **/portal**. They sign in with their email and a 6-digit code, and need no password. They are free and do not count as users.
- **Client**: progress, milestones, site photos, IPCs, variations and instructions.
- **Client's QS**: certifies valuations the company has sent.
- **Client's architect**: approves or rejects variations and issues site instructions (the engineer can issue instructions too).
- **Subcontractor**: their own subcontract and payments, and submits their valuations.

They only see projects they were invited to, and never see workers, materials, payroll or the company's costs.
