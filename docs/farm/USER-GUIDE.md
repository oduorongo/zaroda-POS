# Zaroda Shamba: user guide

Zaroda Shamba runs a farm: animals and flocks, milk and eggs, crops, feed, workers, buyers, the till and the books. This guide follows a farm from its first day. Everyone signs in at **app.zarodashop.com**. Herdsmen and field staff can do all of their work on a phone.

## 1. Start

1. Go to **shamba.zarodasolutions.app** and press **Start free**. Enter your farm's name, county, your name, email and a password. You get 14 days of the Farmer plan free; no M-Pesa is needed to start.
2. You land on the **Farm** home. The plan card shows your limits (acres, users, head of livestock, birds) and how much of each you use.
3. Press **Edit farm details** to fix the farm name, county, sub-county and ward.

Plans: Kienyeji (free), Mkulima (KES 1,000 a month), Farmer (KES 2,500), Commercial (KES 6,500) and Estate (by quote). A feature that is not on your plan shows an upgrade message, never an empty screen. See **Pricing**.

## 2. Set up the farm

- **Layout:** add the parts of the farm: blocks, fields and paddocks (with acres), cow sheds, pens, poultry houses, ponds, apiaries, stores and the feed room. Parts can sit inside others. Acres of fields and paddocks count toward your plan.
- **Enterprises:** each line of business, such as Dairy herd, Layers, Maize long rains or Feed mixing, and the parts of the farm it uses. Tagging costs and income to enterprises is what gives you profit per enterprise.
- **Staff:** add people in the back office, then give each a farm role: farm manager, herdsman, vet, crops officer, feed miller, storekeeper, sales or auditor. A herdsman or crops officer can be limited to their own sheds or fields; they then see and record only there. Casual workers are not staff (see section 9).
- **Farm inputs and farm products:** the system keeps two lists.
  - **Farm inputs** (Production > Farm inputs) are what **comes into** the farm: seed and seedlings, fertiliser and manure, sprays and chemicals, vet drugs and vaccines, bought feed and feed ingredients. Add each one with what it is counted in (bag, kg, litre, packet) and its cost, then press **Received** for each delivery: how many, what you paid, the supplier and their delivery note. The stock goes up, the cost is averaged, and the delivery is a goods-received note, owed to the supplier until their bill is entered. Something you had already added as a product can be moved here with **Already added it as a product?**
  - **Farm products** (Selling > Farm products & prices) are what the farm **produces**, to sell or to use in making other products: milk, eggs, harvests, honey, and home-mixed feed. These are what you sell at the till and to buyers, and what milk, egg and harvest records go into.
  - Each screen offers the right list: field work and spraying pick from farm inputs; harvests, milk, eggs, other produce and buyer deliveries go into farm products; feed rations can use both (bought ingredients and your own maize).

## 3. Animals and flocks

- **Animals:** add cows, goats, sheep and pigs one by one with ear tag, name, breed, sex, birth date, dam and sire (or the AI bull's code), and where they came from.
- **Flocks and groups:** poultry, fish and small groups are kept as a group with a head count. Every change (placed, born, bought, died, sold, slaughtered, culled, moved) is recorded with its reason, and the count never goes below zero.
- **Selling or losing an animal:** on the animal, press the exit action (sold, died, slaughtered, culled, lost). An animal under a meat withdrawal cannot be sold or slaughtered. A movement permit number is kept for animals leaving the farm.

## 4. Health, treatments and withdrawal

- **Drugs:** add each drug or vaccine with the milk, meat and egg withdrawal days from its label. Prescription-only drugs can be recorded only by the vet or a farm manager.
- **Treatments:** on an animal or group, record sickness, treatment, vaccination, deworming or dipping with the drug, dose, who gave it and the cost. The withdrawal dates are worked out from the drug.
- **Withheld:** a treated animal shows **Withheld** until its milk, meat or eggs may be sold again. Withheld milk and eggs never go into stock, and the till refuses to sell more than is in stock (section 10).
- **Programmes and reminders:** on Mkulima and up, set vaccination, deworming and dipping programmes; what is due appears under Health, with SMS and email reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before, on the day, and once when overdue.
- **Breeding:** record heat, service or AI, pregnancy checks, drying off and births (each calf gets its own record). The expected birth date and drying off are worked out for you.

## 5. Milk and eggs

- **Milk:** pick the day and the milking (morning, midday, evening), type each cow's litres, and choose what the milk was for: for sale, calves, home or poured away. Milk for sale goes into stock for the till. A treated cow's milk is saved as withheld and never reaches stock.
- **Eggs:** each flock's good, cracked and small eggs for the day, feed eaten and birds that died. Good eggs go into stock as trays; deaths come off the flock.
- **Settings** (Yields & produce > Settings): press **Make milk and egg products** once, or choose your own products. Set the price under Products.
- **Yields & produce:** milk, eggs, lay % and deaths by day, week or month, and each cow's litres a day. Record honey, manure, fish and other produce here too, into stock if you sell it.
- A wrong record is **voided** by a manager: it stays visible, marked void, and its stock comes back out.

## 6. Crops

- **New season:** pick the field, the crop and variety, the acres (never more than the field), planting and expected harvest dates, and the seed supplier and lot number (KEPHIS).
- **Field work:** land preparation, planting, weeding, top dressing, spraying, irrigation, scouting. Fertiliser, seed or chemical (from **Farm inputs**) taken from stock comes off stock at its cost; workers and labour cost are kept.
- **Spraying:** the chemical, rate, who sprayed and the **pre-harvest interval** from the label are required. Harvest before the interval ends is refused (Pest Control Products Board).
- **Harvest:** in kg, 90 or 50 kg bags, tonnes, or crates and bunches with their kg. Put it into stock (a **farm product**) if you sell it. Each season shows the yield and cost per acre and per kg.

## 7. Feed

- **Rations** (Mkulima and up): the recipe for a home-mixed feed from stock ingredients (bought ones are **farm inputs**; your own maize or beans are farm products), and the kg one batch makes. The finished feed is a farm product.
- **Mixing:** the feed miller records each batch and the kg weighed out (numbered for tracing, KEBS). Nothing moves in stock until a farm manager **approves** it; then the ingredients come off and the feed goes in at its real cost per kg. An approved batch is never edited: it is reversed.
- **Feeding:** each day's feed per flock, group or cow, from stock at its cost.
- **Overview:** feed cost per litre of milk, per egg and per head per day, and how many days each feed will last. A feed with less than a week left is flagged in orange on the Feed page and the farm home.

## 8. Selling at the till

- Open the till (**Selling > Open the till**, or pos.zarodashop.com), tap your PIN, pick Fresh milk or Eggs, type the litres or trays and take cash or M-Pesa. The receipt prints (and goes to KRA when eTIMS is on) and the stock goes down.
- **Products & prices**, **Sales**, **Stock** and **Buyers** are under Selling. The farm home shows the milk and eggs ready to sell.
- The till will not sell more milk or eggs than are fit for sale. A sale rung up while the till is offline is checked when it syncs.

## 9. Workers and wages

Workers & wages is on the Farmer plan and up.

- **Workers:** casual workers with their M-Pesa number and pay a day. They never sign in and never count as users.
- **Muster roll:** each day, mark every worker present, half day or absent, with the task, the part of the farm and the enterprise.
- **Pay runs:** choose the days (up to 31). Pay is worked out from the muster roll with NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy and PAYE at the rates in force. Someone other than the person who prepared it approves it (a farm with nobody else lets the owner approve). Then pay by M-Pesa from the farm's own payout account, mark it paid by M-Pesa sent by hand (with its code), or paid in cash. Each worker gets an SMS payslip.
- **Payout account:** the farm's own Safaricom Daraja B2C shortcode, initiator and keys; they are stored encrypted.
- Paid days are fixed and only draft pay runs can be deleted.

## 10. Buyers and credit

Buyers & credit is on the Mkulima plan and up.

- **Buyers:** dairy co-ops, processors, hotels, schools and regular customers, with your member number, a credit limit and how many days they take to pay.
- **Deliver:** produce on credit, for example 20 L of milk to the co-op on the morning collection with its slip number. It leaves stock. Going past a buyer's limit needs a farm manager.
- **Payment:** money received by M-Pesa, bank, cheque (reference required) or cash.
- **What each buyer owes** by age (0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and over 90 days; payments clear the oldest first) and a **printable statement** with the balance brought forward.
- **Sales by enterprise:** tag each product to an enterprise (Fresh milk to Dairy herd) so till sales and deliveries count toward it.

## 11. Reports and books

Reports are for farm managers and auditors. The registers come on every plan; profit, wages, debtors and Excel downloads come with full reports (Farmer and up).

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Profit per enterprise | Income (till, credit, animals sold) less feed, vet and drugs, labour, field inputs and work, and animals bought |
| Cost per litre, egg, kg and acre | Each enterprise's cost and income per unit it produced, and each crop season per kg and per acre |
| Herd register | Every animal and flock, with breed, sex, birth, place, dam, sire, last weight and withdrawal |
| Breeding calendar | Births due, drying off, pregnancy checks and expected heats |
| Health and withdrawal register | Every treatment with drug, dose, vet, cost and the milk, meat and egg withdrawal dates |
| Crop report | Each season's yield and cost per acre and per kg, safe-harvest date and seed lot |
| Wages by worker | Days, gross, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, PAYE and net, and what is still unpaid |
| Buyers who owe the farm | Each buyer's balance by age |
| Animal valuation and suggested prices | Every animal and flock: what it cost, its weight and production value, the suggested price and the farm's total stock value |

**Download Excel** writes the report to a spreadsheet. **Download PDF** saves it as a PDF and **Print** opens it to print. Every PDF carries the farm's name at the top, the report and period, and who prepared it and when, with page numbers. The same PDF and Print buttons are on buyer statements, pay runs, sales by enterprise, and every Accounts statement (profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, cash flow, aged debtors and creditors, VAT return, and an account's statement).

**Valuation (livestock as assets, IAS 41):** under Money > Valuation, and on each animal's and flock's page. An animal that has been weighed is valued at its **fair value**: its last weight x the market price per kg for its type, less the costs to sell (transport, commission; 5% to start). One not yet weighed has no market price, so it is valued **at cost**: its bought price (or the born value for its type) plus the feed and vet spent on it. Past the prime age for its type the value drops by the yearly rate (15% to start), by at most half. Flocks are valued per head and in total. **Milk, eggs and young are income, never part of an animal's value**: they are shown apart under "Is it paying its way?" with the profit so far (produce + young + value today - what it cost). The asking price is the market value, never below cost + your margin. The farm's total livestock value is carried in its books as **Livestock (1300)**: the herd already there when this started is owner's capital, and each change in its value after that is **Change in livestock value (4300)**, a gain or loss of the year. Farm managers set the margin, the costs to sell, and each type's born value, market price per kg, prime age and yearly drop.

**Accounts (books):** paid wages, credit deliveries, buyer payments, feed fed and field inputs used post to the farm's own general ledger by themselves, next to the till sales. Farms get three extra accounts: Feed used, Seed, fertiliser and chemicals used, and Farm labour. A voided record is reversed in the books.

## 12. Corrections, records and privacy

- Husbandry and money records are never deleted. A manager voids a wrong one with a reason; it stays visible, and its stock and balances are reversed.
- Every create, change, approval and void is in the audit log: who, what and when.
- Each farm sees only its own records. Workers' and buyers' details are kept private under the Data Protection Act 2019.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Farm owner | Everything |
| Farm manager | Everything except the plan: approve feed batches, pay runs and credit past limits; void records; reports |
| Herdsman | Milk, eggs, feeding, muster, deliveries, health checks and weights in their sheds |
| Vet | Treatments (including prescription-only drugs), vaccinations and pregnancy checks |
| Crops officer | Crop seasons, field work, sprays and harvests in their fields |
| Feed miller | Mixing batches and feeding |
| Storekeeper | Stock, feeding and the muster roll |
| Sales | Deliveries, payments and buyers |
| Auditor | Reads everything and the reports; changes nothing |
