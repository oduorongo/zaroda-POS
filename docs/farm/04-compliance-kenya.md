# 04 — Zaroda Shamba: Kenyan Rules It Must Respect

Rules that law or regulators can change are SETTINGS with an effective date (`farm_settings` / tenant settings), never
hard-coded.

| Area | What Shamba does |
|---|---|
| Veterinary drugs (Veterinary Surgeons and Para-professionals Act; Pharmacy and Poisons Board) | Every treatment records the drug, dose and who gave it; prescription-only drugs need a vet name. Withdrawal periods come from the drug's label (entered on the drug) and block sale of milk/meat/eggs until they end. |
| Milk (Kenya Dairy Board, Dairy Industry Regulations) | Milk from treated cows is recorded as withheld and cannot be sold; delivery records to co-ops/processors keep litres and receipts. |
| Animal movement (Animal Diseases Act) | Movements off the farm record the movement permit number and destination; sales of cattle record buyer and permit. |
| Pesticides (Pest Control Products Board) | Spraying records product, rate, who sprayed and the pre-harvest interval; harvest before the interval ends is refused. |
| Seed (KEPHIS) | Seed lots record supplier and certification number. |
| Feeds (KEBS KS standards) | Rations record ingredients and batch numbers so a feed batch can be traced. |
| Tax | VAT, withholding and similar rates live in tax settings; eTIMS invoices for sales where the farm is VAT-registered. |
| Workers | Casual wages, SHIF, NSSF and Housing Levy rates from the shared statutory settings with effective dates. |
| Data | Personal data of workers and buyers handled under the Data Protection Act 2019; files kept private. |

Documents with expiry (vet licences, permits, certifications) get alerts at 60/30/7 days.
