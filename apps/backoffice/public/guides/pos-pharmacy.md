## How it works for a pharmacy

A pharmacy sells medicine that **expires** and some that needs a **prescription**. Zaroda keeps each medicine in **batches** with their expiry dates and asks for prescription details at the till for medicines that need them.

### Set up (once)
1. **Products**: add each medicine with its tax class, price and cost. Turn on **batches** for medicines with expiry dates.
2. **Pharmacy**: mark the medicines that are **controlled** or need a prescription, and their schedule.
3. **Inventory**: receive stock **by batch**, entering each batch's expiry date.

### At the till
- When a medicine has batches, the till asks **which batch** to sell from; sell the one that expires first.
- A prescription medicine needs the **prescription details** before the sale completes. This needs an internet connection.

### Keeping stock safe
- Check **Inventory** for batches close to expiry and sell them first.
- Expired or damaged stock is written off under **Waste**, with the reason, so it shows in your reports and profit.
- Medicine from your suppliers can be received through **Purchase orders**, so the price you paid becomes the cost.
