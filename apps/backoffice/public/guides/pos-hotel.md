## How it works for a hotel

A hotel runs on **rooms and stays**: a guest books, checks in, runs up a bill (the **folio**) for room nights, meals and drinks, and settles it at check-out. Zaroda keeps the front desk, rooms and housekeeping, the restaurant and bar, and the money in one place.

### Set up (once)
1. **Rooms & housekeeping → Room types & photos**: each kind of room (Single, Double, Suite) with its usual price, how many guests it takes, and photos for the marketplace.
2. **Rooms & housekeeping → Rooms**: each actual room (101, 102...) and its type and floor. You can add a run of rooms at once (101 to 110).
3. **Rates & rules**: the price list used by both the front desk and the marketplace:
   - **seasons** (high season prices for set dates) and **weekend** prices;
   - charges for **extra adults and children**, and **meal plans** (bed and breakfast, half board, full board) per person per night;
   - **room taxes** by name (VAT, tourism levy), added on top of the room price;
   - your **cancellation policy** (how many days before arrival a guest can cancel for free, and what share you keep after that);
   - the time of the **night audit** and whether guests get texts.
4. Each room type gets a product named **Room: <type>** on Products; its tax class sets the tax.

### A stay, step by step
1. **Book**: on **Front desk → New booking**, or the guest books on the Zaroda marketplace and pays a deposit by M-Pesa. Both use the same rooms, so a room is never sold twice.
2. **Check in**: choose the room, record the guest's details in the **guest register** (ID number kept private) and take a deposit if you like. All the nights are put on the folio.
3. **During the stay**: anything the guest buys at the restaurant or bar can be **charged to the room** from the till, and goes on the folio. Take payments on account at any time. **Change stay** adds or removes nights or changes the rate; **Move room** moves the guest.
4. **Check out**: the folio shows every night, charge, tax and payment. Take the balance (cash, M-Pesa, card) and check out. The stay becomes a sale, so it is in your Sales, reports, books and KRA eTIMS.
5. **Housekeeping**: rooms that are checked out show as needing cleaning until housekeeping marks them clean.

**Cancel** and **No-show** follow your cancellation policy. Guests can get a reminder text before arrival and a thank-you after.

### Close the day (night audit)
Each night (automatically at the time you set, or **Close the day** on Front desk), Zaroda charges guests who stayed past their check-out, marks unpaid arrivals who never came as no-shows, and locks the day's figures.

### The hotel screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Front desk** | Today's arrivals, guests in the house and departures; the room grid; new booking, check-in, check-out, folio, payments, change stay, move room, cancel, no-show, close the day |
| **Rooms & housekeeping** | Rooms, room types and photos, bookings, availability, and housekeeping |
| **Rates & rules** | Seasons, weekend prices, extra guests, meal plans, room taxes, cancellation policy, texts |
| **Hotel reports** | Occupancy, revenue by source, arrivals, departures, guests in house, who owes, and cancellations & no-shows; each downloads as a spreadsheet |
| **Restaurant** (optional) | Tables, kitchen and open bills, as for a restaurant |

Cashiers and supervisors use the **till** (PIN) to sell and charge to rooms; the Front desk screen is for managers and the owner.

### Common questions for hotels
**I cannot check a guest in.** The hotel needs its rooms added under Rooms & housekeeping → Rooms, and the room must be free and clean.

**"Charge to room" is refused at the till.** The guest must be checked in. Charges made while the room was not in use are refused.

**A guest's bill looks wrong.** Open the folio: each night, charge, tax and payment is listed with its date. A manager can make an adjustment to take money off; extra charges go through the till as a charge to the room.
