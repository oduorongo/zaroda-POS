## How it works for a salon

A salon sells **services by appointment** (and often products too). Clients book a time with a stylist, the service is done, and it is paid at the till like any sale.

### Set up (once)
1. **Products**: add each **service** (haircut, braiding, manicure) with its price, and any products you sell (shampoo, hair pieces) with their stock.
2. **Staff**: add your stylists, so bookings and sales show who did the work.

### Day to day
1. **Bookings**: each appointment with the client, the service, the time and the stylist. It moves from **scheduled** to **confirmed**, **in progress** and **completed**, or **cancelled** / **no-show**.
2. When the service is done, sell it at the **till** and take cash or M-Pesa. Add the client as a **customer** so they earn loyalty points.
3. **Reports → by cashier** shows what each person sold.
