# ZARODA RENTALS: user guide

ZARODA RENTALS runs rental property for Kenyan landlords and agents: properties and units, tenants and leases, monthly rent invoices, M-Pesa rent, receipts, reminders, repairs, meters, caretakers, house adverts and the books.
Open it at **rentals.zarodasolutions.app**. It works on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

There are three kinds of people in ZARODA RENTALS:
- **The landlord** (the owner of the account, and any managers they add) sees and does everything.
- **A caretaker** sees only the properties the landlord gives them, and only rent, repairs and meters.
- **Tenants** never sign in here. They use their own **rent account** (the tenant portal) to see what they owe, pay by M-Pesa and report repairs.

---

## Getting started

### Create your account
1. Open **rentals.zarodasolutions.app** and tap **Start free**.
2. Enter your business or property name, your name, email, a password (at least 8 characters) and your county.
3. Tap **Create my account**. You are signed in and taken to **Rentals**.

You start on the free **Bure** plan: properties, units, tenants, leases and monthly rent invoices for up to 5 occupied units, for as long as you like. No M-Pesa payment is needed to start. Some features need a paid plan; each section below says which, and the full table is under **Plans and paying Zaroda**.

### Signing in
Open **rentals.zarodasolutions.app/login** and sign in with your email and password. A caretaker signs in the same way with the details the landlord gave them.

If you also run a shop on Zaroda POS with a different account, sign in to that on the Zaroda back office instead; the rentals site only accepts rentals accounts.

### The Rentals screen
At the top you always see three figures:
- **Units**: how many units you have.
- **Occupied**: how many have a tenant.
- **Rent per month**: the total monthly rent of all active leases.

Below them are the tabs: **Properties, Tenants, Leases, Rent, Expenses, Maintenance, Meters, Reports, Houses to let, Caretakers** and **Plan**. The rest of this guide goes through them in the order you will usually use them.

The top menu also has **Accounts** (your books), **Staff** (people who can sign in) and **Guide** (this page).

## Step 1: Properties and units

A **property** is a building or plot (for example *Kilimani Apartments*). A **unit** is one house, room or shop in it (*A4*, *Shop 2*).

### Add a property
On **Properties**, type the property name and its address or location, then tap **Add property**.

### Add one unit
Choose the property, type the unit name, its **monthly rent** and its **deposit**, then tap **Add unit**.

### Add many units at once
Under **Add many units at once**:
1. Choose the property.
2. Type the rent and deposit (the same for all of them; you can change any one later on its lease).
3. Type the unit names as a range, like **A1-A20**, or as a list separated by commas, like **A1, A2, Shop 3**.
4. Check the preview line (*Will add: A1, A2, ...*) and tap **Add 20 units**.

Each property then shows its units as tiles: **green** for occupied (with the tenant's name) and **amber** for vacant.

---

## Step 2: Tenants

On **Tenants**, enter the tenant's **full name**, **phone**, **email** and **ID number**, then tap **Add tenant**.

To move them in straight away, choose a vacant unit under **Give a unit now**, then set the **move-in date** and the **day of the month rent is due** (1 to 28). This starts their lease at the same time.

For each tenant you can:
- **Give unit** (or **Add another unit** if they rent more than one).
- **Invite to portal** (Starter and above): emails them a link to their own rent account. They need an email address on their record for this.
- **Add email / Change email** and **Add phone / Change phone**. The phone number is used for M-Pesa prompts and SMS reminders; the email for reminders and the portal invite.

Tip: add a phone and an email for every tenant. Reminders, the portal and M-Pesa prompts all depend on them.

---

## Step 3: Leases

A **lease** joins a tenant to a unit. Rent is only billed for active leases.

### Start a lease
On **Leases**:
1. Choose a **vacant unit** (its rent and deposit fill in for you).
2. Choose the **tenant**.
3. Set the **lease start date**, the **monthly rent**, the **deposit** and the **day rent is due** (1 to 28).
4. Tap **Start lease**.

What happens next:
- A **deposit invoice** is made on the start date (if there is a deposit).
- A **rent invoice** is made every month for the tenant, due on their due day. In the month they move in, rent is never due before the move-in date.
- If you start a lease in the past, the invoices for the months already passed are made too, so the tenant's balance is right from day one.

### End a lease (moving out)
Tap **End lease** on the tenant's line. You are asked:
1. How much of the **deposit you are handing back** (enter 0 for none).
2. If you are refunding some, **how**: cash, bank or M-Pesa.

The unit becomes vacant and rent stops being billed. The refund, and anything kept from the deposit, are recorded in your books.

## Step 4: Collecting rent (the Rent tab)

### The figures at the top
- **Owed by tenants**: everything still unpaid.
- **Overdue**: the part that is past its due date (in red).
- **Tenants owing**: how many of your tenants owe anything.

### Who owes what
This list shows every active lease: the tenant and their phone, the unit, the rent, what they **owe** (or **Paid up**), what is **overdue**, any **credit** (money paid in advance) and when they **last paid**. On each line you can:
- **Record payment**: for money you received in cash, by bank, or by M-Pesa straight to your number or Paybill.
- **Ask by M-Pesa**: send an M-Pesa prompt to the tenant's phone (Pay-as-you-collect, once automatic M-Pesa is set up, below).
- **+ Charge**: add a one-off charge (water, a repair, a late fee).
- **Remind**: send a reminder now, by email and (if set up) SMS (Starter and above).
- **Statement**: open the tenant's statement to print or send (Starter and above).

### Record a payment by hand
Tap **Record payment**, then enter the **amount**, **how** it was paid (M-Pesa, cash, bank or other), the **M-Pesa code or reference**, the **date received** (blank means today) and a note if you like. Tap **Save payment**.

The payment pays off the tenant's **oldest** unpaid invoice first, then the next. Anything extra stays as **credit** and is used on their next invoice. A **receipt** number is given at once; on Starter and above tap **Print receipt** to print it.

### Payment history and receipts
**Payment history** lists every payment with its receipt number. Tap **Receipt** to print it or save it as PDF (Starter and above). If a payment was entered by mistake, tap **Void** and give the reason: it stops counting as paid and the tenant's balance goes back up. The voided receipt stays in the list, crossed out, so nothing disappears.

### Statements
**Statement** shows every invoice and payment for a lease with the running balance. Print it, or choose "Save as PDF" in the print window to send it.

## Step 5: Automatic M-Pesa

With automatic M-Pesa, the tenant gets a prompt on their phone, enters their PIN, and the payment is **recorded, matched to the right tenant and receipted by itself**. The money goes straight to **your own** account, never to Zaroda. Automatic M-Pesa comes with the **Pay-as-you-collect** plan; on other plans you record M-Pesa payments by hand with their code.

### Set it up
On **Rent**, open **Automatic M-Pesa** and tap **Set up**. Choose one:
- **Tuma**: enter your Tuma account email and Tuma API key, then **Connect Tuma** and **Use Tuma for rent**.
- **My own Paybill / Till**: choose **Paybill** or **Till (Buy Goods)**, enter the Paybill number (or the store and till numbers), and your Safaricom **Daraja consumer key**, **consumer secret** and **Lipa na M-Pesa passkey**. Choose **Live** (or the Safaricom test system while trying it out). Tap **Connect my Paybill / Till**, then **Use my Paybill / Till for rent**.

Your keys are stored encrypted and are never shown again. You can **Replace** or **Disconnect** them at any time.

### Using it
- On **Who owes what**, tap **Ask by M-Pesa**, check the amount and the tenant's Safaricom number, and tap **Send M-Pesa prompt**.
- Tenants can also pay from their own rent account at any time.
- Prompts still waiting show under **M-Pesa prompts waiting or needing attention**. If a tenant says they paid but it has not shown, tap **Check now**. A payment that does not match the prompt is marked **needs a look** so you can check it.

Money a tenant sends to your Paybill or number **without** a prompt cannot be matched by itself: record it with **Record payment** and its M-Pesa code.

## Step 6: Rent reminders

Reminders come with **Starter** and above. On **Rent**, under **Rent reminders**:
- Tick **Send reminders automatically**. Each tenant who owes is reminded **3 days before** the due date, **on** the due date, **3 days late** and **10 days late**.
- Tick **Also send an SMS** to text them as well as email them.
- Type **how tenants should pay** (for example *Paybill 123456, account = your unit number*). This goes in every reminder.

Reminders only go out between **8am and 8pm**, and only to tenants with an email (and a phone, for SMS) on their record. You can still send one by hand any time with **Remind**.

## Expenses

On **Expenses**, record what each property costs you: choose the **property**, the **kind** (repairs, utilities, rates and taxes, insurance, caretaker, management fee, other), what it was for, the **amount**, who you **paid** and the **date**. The list shows the total. Expenses feed the property's **net income** on **Reports**.

---

## Maintenance (repairs)

On **Maintenance**, log anything that needs fixing:
1. Choose the **property** and the **unit**, or **Whole property / common area**.
2. Describe the problem (*Leaking tap*) and add detail if you like.
3. Set the priority: **Low**, **Normal** or **Urgent**, then **Log request**.

Move each request along as it is handled: **Open → In progress → Done** (or **Cancelled**). When it is fixed you can record what it **cost** and who did it; the cost is kept with the property's expenses.

Tenants can report problems from their rent account too: these appear here straight away. Tick **show closed** to see finished work.

---

## Meters (water and electricity)

On **Meters**, enter each unit's reading every month:
1. Choose the **unit** and **Water** or **Electricity**.
2. Enter the **reading**, your **rate per unit**, and the date (blank means today).
3. Tap **Save reading**.

The units used since the last reading, times your rate, are **added to the tenant's balance** as a charge. A meter's **first** reading is only the starting point; to bill that first month, enter the **previous reading** as well.

---

## Reports

On **Reports**, choose a **month** to see, for each property: units **occupied**, rent **charged**, rent **collected**, **expenses**, **net income** and **arrears** (all time). Tap **Print report** for a printable copy (or "Save as PDF").

---

## Houses to let (adverts)

Advertise a vacant unit on Zaroda's **houses to let** listings, where people looking for a house can find it and call you. Each advert costs **KES 100 for 30 days**.

1. On **Houses to let**, choose the vacant unit and fill in the advert: a short title (*Spacious bedsitter near the stage*), the location, the rent, the details and the phone number people should call.
2. **Save**, then add **photos** (adverts with photos get far more calls) and choose the **cover photo**.
3. **Pay** KES 100 by M-Pesa to publish it. To pay for several at once, tick them and use **Pay for several adverts at once**.

**Copy these details to your other empty units** saves typing when you have several similar vacant units. You can **pause** an advert (hidden from the public) or **remove** it with its photos. When the unit is let, take the advert down.

## Short stays (Airbnb-style lets)

Let a furnished unit **by the night** instead of on a lease: bookings you take yourself, and bookings from **Airbnb** and **Booking.com**, all on one calendar with no double bookings. Short stays come with **Starter** and **Pay-as-you-collect**. A unit let by the night cannot also have a lease or a Houses to let advert.

### Set up a unit
1. Open **Short stays**, then **Units & calendars**, and press **Let by the night** on a vacant unit.
2. Enter the **price per night**, a **Friday & Saturday** price if it is higher, the **cleaning fee** per stay, the fewest nights, the most guests, check-in and check-out times, **directions**, the **door or lockbox code**, and the **cleaner's** name and phone.
3. If you manage the unit for someone else, choose its **owner** (add owners under **Owners & statements**).

### Keep Airbnb and Booking.com in step
Each site and ZARODA swap a **calendar link** (iCal), so a booking made in one place blocks the dates in the others.
- **Into ZARODA:** in Airbnb (Calendar > Availability > Connect calendars > Export) or Booking.com (Rates & availability > Sync calendars > Export), copy the link, then on the unit press **Link calendar** and paste it. It is read straight away and then every hour. **Read now** reads it again.
- **Out of ZARODA:** press **Copy link for Airbnb** (or **for Booking.com**) and paste it into that site's **Import calendar**. Each site gets its own link, which leaves out that site's own bookings.
- **New links** makes fresh links and stops the old ones (if a link was shared by mistake). Paste the new ones into the sites.

### The calendar
**Calendar** shows each unit night by night: navy for your own (direct) bookings, orange for Airbnb, gold for Booking.com, striped for dates taken on a site or blocked by you. A dot means the guest still owes money; the broom means a cleaning is due. A **red outline** means a booking-site booking clashes with one here: sort it out on the site at once.
- Tap a free night to **book** it, or choose to **block** it (owner staying, repairs). Tap a blocked night to free it.
- Tap a booking to open it.

### Bookings
**New booking** asks for the unit, dates (check-out is the day they leave), guest, phone and email. For a direct booking the price comes from the unit's rates and is shown before you save; the fewest nights and most guests are checked. For an Airbnb or Booking.com booking, type the **price of the nights** and the **site's fee** as the site shows them, and its confirmation code.

Open a booking to:
- **Check in** (from the arrival day) and **Check out**. Leaving early moves the cleaning to today.
- **Change dates**; the price is worked out again for direct bookings.
- Mark a **No-show** (from the arrival day), or **Cancel** a booking not yet checked in (owners and managers only). Cancelled dates can be booked again.
- **Record a payment** (cash, M-Pesa with its code, bank, card, or **Paid out by Airbnb / Booking.com**), each with a receipt number, or a **refund**. A booking site pays you the price less its fee, so that fee is not shown as owing.

### Cleaning
Every booking makes a cleaning job for its check-out day. **Cleaning** lists the next 7 days, overdue first, with when the next guest arrives. The caretaker or cleaner taps **Cleaned** and can add a note (linen changed, something broken). Add a one-off job (deep clean) at the bottom.

### Guest messages
For direct bookings the guest gets a **confirmation**, then the **day before** arrival the directions and door code, and a **thank-you** after check-out. The **cleaner** is texted when a guest checks out. Tick **Text guests and the cleaner** on **Units & calendars** to use text messages; email is used whenever the guest gave an address. Airbnb and Booking.com guests are messaged by those sites.

### Owners and statements
Under **Owners & statements**, add each owner with their **commission %**. The **monthly statement** shows, per unit: nights let, how full it was, income from nights and cleaning, the booking sites' fees, your commission (on income after those fees), expenses recorded against the unit that month (on the **Expenses** tab), and what is **payable to the owner**. A stay across two months is shared night by night. Choose an owner or **My own units**, then **Download CSV** or **Print**.

### Caretakers on short stays
A caretaker sees the calendar, bookings and cleaning for their properties: they can book guests, check them in and out, record payments and mark cleanings done. Prices, calendar links, owners, statements, cancelling and voiding payments stay with you.

## Caretakers

A caretaker can collect and record rent, log repairs and enter meter readings for the properties you choose, without seeing the rest of your business. Caretakers come with **Starter** (1) and **Pay-as-you-collect** (up to 5).

1. Open **Staff** (top menu) and add the person with the role **Caretaker**.
2. On **Caretakers**, tick the **properties** they look after. Until you tick at least one, they cannot see anything.

A caretaker sees only **Rent**, **Short stays**, **Maintenance** and **Meters**, and only for their properties. They can record payments and see statements and receipts, but cannot void payments, add charges, send reminders, change M-Pesa settings or see expenses, reports or your plan.

How many caretakers you can have depends on your plan (see **Plans and paying Zaroda**).

---

## Accounts (your books)

Open **Accounts** in the top menu. Your books keep themselves: every rent and deposit invoice, payment, voided receipt and lease ending is posted for you, so **rent owed by tenants**, **rent received**, **deposits held** and **deposits refunded or kept** are always up to date.

From **Statements** you get the **profit and loss**, **balance sheet**, **trial balance** and more, each downloadable. Opening balances and any corrections are entered as **journals**. See the Accounts screens for details.

## Plans and paying Zaroda

### The plans
| Plan | Price | Occupied units | Caretakers | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bure** | Free | Up to 5 | None | Tenant records and monthly rent invoices |
| **Starter** | KES 1,000 a month, or KES 10,000 a year (12 months for the price of 10) | Up to 30 | 1 | Tenant portal, printable receipts and statements, rent reminders, short stays |
| **Pay-as-you-collect** | 1% of rent matched through the app, no monthly fee | Unlimited | Up to 5 | Everything in Starter, plus automatic M-Pesa: prompts from you, payments from the tenant portal, matched and receipted for you |

Change plan on the **Plan** tab. Pay-as-you-collect needs automatic M-Pesa connected first (Rent tab). If you have more occupied units than a plan covers, it tells you before you switch.

### Your Zaroda invoice
On a paid plan, Zaroda's invoice is issued on the **1st** of the month and is due on the **15th**. You get reminders by email before then. Pay it by M-Pesa from the **Plan** tab: the payment is confirmed by itself.

An invoice still unpaid after the 15th may make your account **read-only**: you can still see everything, and pay, but cannot add or change anything until it is paid. Nothing is ever deleted.

The first landlords to pay keep their launch price for 12 months.

## For tenants: your rent account

Your landlord uses ZARODA RENTALS. You have your own **rent account** where you can:
- see **what you owe** and every invoice,
- **pay by M-Pesa** (if your landlord offers it),
- see **every payment you have made** and open your **receipts** and **statement**,
- tell your landlord about **anything that needs fixing**.

### Signing in
Open the link in the invite email from your landlord (*Invite to portal*). Enter your **email** and you get a **6-digit code**; where texts are set up you can use your **phone number** instead and get the code by SMS. There is no password. If you have no account yet, ask your landlord to add your email or phone number.

### Paying
Enter the **amount** and your **M-Pesa number** (leave it blank to use the number on file), tap pay, and enter your PIN on your phone. Your payment is recorded and receipted straight away. If your landlord does not take M-Pesa in the app, the page shows how to pay them directly.

### Reporting a repair
Under **Something needs fixing?**, describe the problem and send it. Your landlord sees it at once.

## Common questions

**A tenant paid but it is not showing.** If they paid through a prompt, open **M-Pesa prompts waiting** on the Rent tab and tap **Check now**. If they sent money straight to your number or Paybill, record it with **Record payment** and the M-Pesa code.

**I entered a payment twice.** Open **Payment history**, tap **Void** on the wrong one and give the reason.

**A tenant paid more than they owe.** The extra is kept as **credit** and used on their next invoice automatically.

**Rent for a new tenant started in the past.** Start the lease with the real start date; the earlier months' invoices are made for you.

**The rent changed.** End the old lease and start a new one with the new rent from the date it changes, or set the right rent when starting the lease.

**Reminders are not going out.** Check you are on Starter or above, that reminders are ticked on the Rent tab, that the tenant has an email (and a phone for SMS), and remember they only go out between 8am and 8pm.

**My caretaker cannot see anything.** Tick their properties on the **Caretakers** tab.

**The screen says "Upgrade" or my account is read-only.** Open **Plan**: either your plan does not include that feature or unit count, or a Zaroda invoice is overdue. Pay it and everything opens again at once.

**Where do I get help?** Contact Zaroda Solutions from the Zaroda website.
