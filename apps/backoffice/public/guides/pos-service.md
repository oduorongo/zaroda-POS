## How it works for a service business

You do **jobs** for customers (repairs, servicing, installations) using your time and sometimes **parts** from stock. Zaroda follows each job from the day it comes in to the day it is paid.

### Set up (once)
1. **Products**: add your **parts** (with stock and cost) and your **labour** charges (as items with a price).
2. **Staff**: add the people who do the work.

### A job, step by step
1. **Job Orders → Open a job**: the branch, what it is for (a vehicle's plate, a machine, a route) and what needs doing.
2. Move it along as work goes on: **open** → **in progress** → **waiting for parts** (if you are) → **completed**, or **cancelled**.
3. **Invoice** the job: the parts used come out of stock and the parts and labour become a sale, paid at the till by cash or M-Pesa. The job shows as **invoiced**.
