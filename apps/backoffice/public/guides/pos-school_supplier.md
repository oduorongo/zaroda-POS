## How it works with schools

As a school supplier you sell to schools **on credit**: a school orders, you deliver, you invoice, and the school pays later (by cheque, bank transfer or M-Pesa). Zaroda keeps the whole trail for you: order → delivery note → invoice → payment → receipt → statement.

The school never sees your back office. Each school gets its own **sign-in to your ordering page**, where it sees your school price list, places orders, follows deliveries and sees what it owes.

### The flow, step by step
1. **Add the school** (Schools): its name, contact person, phone, email, **credit days** (how long it has to pay) and, if you like, a **credit limit**. Zaroda gives the school a **PIN**, shown once: share it with the school together with your **ordering link** (shown on Schools). A school you supply under a tender or contract is still added here first.
2. **Put items on the school price list** (School price list): choose which products schools can order and, if it differs from your shop price, the **school price**. Prices are before VAT; the VAT comes from each product's tax class. Schools only see prices after they sign in.
3. **The school orders**: it signs in with its phone and PIN, picks the items and quantities and gives its own **LPO number**. You and your managers get an alert and an email.
4. **Confirm the order** (School orders → Waiting): check the LPO (tick **LPO received**), and confirm. You may lower a quantity you cannot supply. The **promised to others** column shows stock already promised to earlier orders.
5. **Deliver**: you can deliver everything at once or **in parts**. Each delivery takes the goods out of stock and makes its own **delivery note (DN-)** and **invoice (INV-)**, due after the school's credit days. Print the delivery note for the school to sign; the school can also **confirm receipt** in its portal. If the rest will never come, **close the balance**.
6. **Get paid** (Invoices & payments → Payments): record the cheque, transfer, M-Pesa or cash. A payment may be part of what is owed: it pays the **oldest invoice first** (or the invoices you choose), anything extra stays as **credit**, and a **receipt (RC-)** is made. A bounced cheque is reversed in one step.
7. **Send a statement**: each school's statement shows its invoices, payments, credit notes and how old the balance is. The school sees the same in its portal.

Every invoice is also recorded as a sale (payment **On account**), so your Sales, Reports, Profit & loss, books and KRA eTIMS all include school business.

### The school screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Schools** | Your schools, their PINs, credit days and limits, retention %, and your letterhead (address, phone, email, invoice footer) printed on every document |
| **School price list** | What schools can order and what they pay |
| **School orders** | Orders by stage: **Waiting**, **To deliver**, **Balance to deliver**, **Delivered**, **Cancelled**. Open one to confirm, deliver or print |
| **Quotations** | Quotes (QT-) for a school: draft, send, revise. When the school accepts, it becomes an order at the quoted prices |
| **Tenders** | Tenders you are bidding for: the checklist, what the bid costs and earns, and your compliance documents (tax compliance certificate, business permit) with expiry dates. When you win, one button makes the contract |
| **Tender adverts from schools** | What schools have posted that they want to buy; you get an email for each new one |
| **Contracts** | Running contracts with a school: their value, changes to it, and the orders and invoices under them, so you see what is used and what is left |
| **Invoices & payments** | Invoices, payments, receipts, **credit notes**, who owes you (ageing), **chasing** late payers, **tax withheld**, **retention** and **bad debts** |
| **Cash-flow forecast** | The next 13 weeks: money expected in from schools against what you must pay out |

### Money matters to know
- **Credit limit** only warns you; it never blocks an order.
- **Credit notes (CN-)**: when a school returns goods or a price was wrong, open the invoice and press **Credit note**. It lowers what the school owes, and can put the goods back in stock.
- **Chasing**: overdue invoices, with the date the school promised to pay, any dispute, who is following it up, and notes.
- **Withholding tax**: when a school keeps back tax as it pays, tick **the school also kept back withholding tax** on the payment. Record the certificate when it comes, so you can claim it against your own income tax.
- **Retention**: a school (or a contract) may hold back a percentage of each invoice until a date. It is owed to you but not overdue; record it when it is released.
- **Bad debts**: a provision set aside for very late invoices, and write-offs. A large write-off needs a second person to approve it. The school's statement still shows the debt.
- Printed documents (delivery notes, invoices, receipts, statements, credit notes, quotes) carry your letterhead and the **amount in words**.

### Your plan
School suppliers can pay by **school term** instead of monthly: three instalments a year, due at the start of each term. If an instalment is late past the grace days, the back office becomes **read-only** (you can look but not change anything) until it is paid; paying lifts it at once.

### Common questions for school suppliers
**A school cannot sign in.** Check the phone number on Schools and give a new PIN. Schools sign in on your ordering link, not on zarodashop.com.

**I cannot deliver an order.** Delivering takes the goods out of stock, so you need enough stock in the branch. Receive the goods from your supplier first, or deliver part now.

**A school paid less than the invoice.** Record what came in. The invoice stays open for the balance and shows in Chasing when it is late.
