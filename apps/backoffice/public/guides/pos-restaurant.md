## How it works for a restaurant

A restaurant sells **dishes made from ingredients** at **tables**. Orders go to the **kitchen**, the table runs a **bill** while the diners eat, and it is paid at the end (all at once, in parts, or split between people). Your stock goes down by the ingredients each dish uses, not just the dish.

### Set up (once)
1. **Products**: add your **ingredients** (sold by weight or volume, with their cost) and your **dishes**. Give each dish a **recipe** (Products → Recipe): how much of each ingredient one portion uses. Selling a dish takes its ingredients out of stock and gives you the true cost of the plate.
2. **Tables & Kitchen → Menu & kitchen setup**: your **tables** and your **kitchen stations** (kitchen, grill, bar...), and which station makes each dish.
3. **Table QR codes** (optional): print a QR code for each table. Diners scan it, order and pay by M-Pesa from their phone, with no sign-in.
4. **Reservations** (optional): switch on table reservations so guests can ask for a table on your online shop.

### Service, step by step
1. **Open a bill** for a table on the till. Add dishes as they are ordered; they go to the kitchen at once, or you **hold** a course and send it later.
2. The **kitchen screen** shows each ticket by station. The cook marks it ready and it is served.
3. Changing your mind: a supervisor can **void** a line (with a reason; the kitchen sees it struck through). A bill can be **moved** to another table.
4. **Print or share the bill**, then take payment: the whole bill, **part of it**, **split by person** (each pays for what they had) or **split equally**. Cash, M-Pesa or card.
5. When the diners leave, the table shows **needs cleaning** until it is marked clean.

Orders placed with the table QR code are paid first, then go straight to the kitchen.

### The restaurant screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Tables & Kitchen → Floor & kitchen** | Every table's status (free, occupied, reserved, needs cleaning) and the kitchen tickets |
| **Menu & kitchen setup** | Tables, kitchen stations, and which station makes each dish |
| **Table QR codes** | Print the QR code for each table |
| **Reservations** | Confirm, decline or seat table bookings |
| **Menu engineering** | Which dishes earn the most and sell the most, so you know what to push, re-price or drop |

If you also let rooms, the **Front desk** and **Rooms & housekeeping** screens appear too (see the hotel parts of this guide).

### Common questions for restaurants
**A dish shows as not available online.** A dish with a recipe can only be ordered while there are enough ingredients for it. Receive more stock of the scarcest ingredient.

**My food cost looks wrong.** Check that every ingredient has its cost and every dish has a recipe.

**Two tills tried to take payment for the same table.** Only one can charge each line; the other is told the lines are already being paid.
