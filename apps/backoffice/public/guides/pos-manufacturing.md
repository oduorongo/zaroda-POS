## How it works for production

You **make** what you sell: bread from flour, juice from fruit, soap from oils. Zaroda turns raw materials into finished goods in **production orders**, so your stock and costs stay right.

### Set up (once)
1. **Products**: add your **raw materials** (with their cost) and your **finished goods**.
2. Give each finished good a **recipe** (Products → Recipe): how much of each raw material one unit uses.

### A production run
1. **Production → Raise a production order**: choose the finished good, the branch and how many you are making.
2. **Start** it when work begins.
3. **Complete** it when the goods are made: the raw materials come out of stock and the finished goods go in, at their true cost. **Cancel** an order that will not go ahead.

Buy raw materials through **Purchase orders** so their cost is always up to date, and check **Profit & loss** to see what each product really earns.
